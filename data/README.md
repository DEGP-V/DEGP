The Push-T dataset is regenerated by notebook Cell 1 (seededRandomState(1234), ≈15 min, reproduces exactly) or downloaded as afrozen archive (GitHub Release asset / Zenodo; linked from the mainREADME once published) into data/c2data/.

Collection protocol (paper §3.5)
250 episodes × 200 steps = 50,000 transitions; random persistentagent targets resampled every 10 steps; position-controlled agent(k_p=100, k_v=20, 10 Hz); gym-pusht / pymunk 6.11.1.
36.4% of steps in agent–block contact; 8.0% move the T-block.
Episode-level split: episodes 1–200 train / 201–250 validation(39,800 / 9,950 transition pairs; no pair crosses the split);normalization by train statistics.
